About OU_CPS Lab

The OU_CPS Lab focuses on Cyber-Physical Systems, IoT systems, and machine learning. Our research aims to build intelligent, data-driven systems for smart homes, buildings, and energy infrastructure.


Research Areas

  • Cyber-Physical Systems
  • IoT Systems and Privacy
  • Machine Learning for Sensing and Control
  • Smart Homes and Energy Systems

Selected Projects

  • Gen-HVAC: Learning-based HVAC control across buildings
  • IoT Privacy: Inferring device activity from encrypted traffic
  • Solar Detection: Satellite-based energy system analysis
