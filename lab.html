Li Lab

Cyber-Physical Systems, IoT, Machine Learning, and Privacy

We build data-driven systems for smart homes, buildings, energy systems, and connected environments. Our work combines machine learning with sensing, networking, and system design to improve efficiency, resilience, and privacy in real-world cyber-physical systems.

We are looking for motivated undergraduate, MS, and PhD students interested in cyber-physical systems, IoT, applied machine learning, sensing, and privacy.

Research Areas

Our group studies system and learning problems motivated by real deployment settings.

Cyber-Physical Systems

Learning-enabled control, smart homes and buildings, sensing systems, and real-world deployment.

IoT Privacy and Security

Traffic analysis, user privacy inference, device activity modeling, and privacy-aware system design.

Machine Learning for Energy

Data-driven methods for buildings, grids, distributed energy systems, and large-scale sensing.
