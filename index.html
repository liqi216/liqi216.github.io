About Me

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Oklahoma. I received my Ph.D from Colorado School of Mines under the supervision of Dr. Dong Chen.


My research interests lie at the intersection of Machine Learning, Image Processing, and Cyber-Physical Energy Systems. Recently, I have focused on integrating deep learning with image processing techniques to develop computer systems that enhance the energy efficiency of Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS), particularly in the contexts of smart homes, buildings, grids, and cities.


I am actively seeking highly motivated undergraduate and graduate researchers to join my lab! If you’re interested, please feel free to email me!
